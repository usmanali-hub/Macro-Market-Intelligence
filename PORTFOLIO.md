## Analyst approach

I structure analysis as:

**Question → Data → Validation → Metric → Segmentation → Evidence → Uncertainty → Decision**

The goal is not to produce charts for their own sake. Each project starts with a business question and ends with an analytical interpretation, limitation, or next question.

## Portfolio design

### 01 — Macro Market Intelligence
Transforms public economic indicators into structured market context using time-series analysis, derived features, correlations, and heuristic regime classification.

### 02 — Trading Risk & Performance Analytics
Separates headline trading returns from drawdown, tail risk, concentration, recovery, and segment-level performance using synthetic trade data.

### 03 — Customer Support Analytics
Connects ticket demand, response time, resolution time, SLA attainment, escalation, reopening, and CSAT to operational decisions using synthetic support data.

## Data integrity

The financial trading and customer-support projects use synthetic data. The macro project uses public economic data. No private customer, employer, brokerage, or account records are included.

## Reproducibility

Each repository contains its own documented pipeline, analytical scripts, SQL queries, tests/quality checks, methodology notes, and executive reporting layer.
