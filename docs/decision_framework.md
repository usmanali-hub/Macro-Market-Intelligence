# Decision Framework

## 1. What happened?
Track direction, magnitude, and persistence of inflation, growth, unemployment, policy rates, and the 10-year Treasury yield.

## 2. Why might it matter?
Compare momentum and relationships across indicators rather than relying on one release. Use rolling changes, standardized values, and the yield-curve proxy to identify changing macro conditions.

## 3. What should be investigated next?
Prioritize regime transitions, unusually large three-month changes, and relationships that remain stable across different periods.

## 4. Decision guardrails
- Treat heuristic regimes as descriptive, not predictive.
- Validate important conclusions against the underlying observations.
- Correlation does not imply causation.
- Do not convert this research workflow into an investment recommendation without additional validation and domain review.
